The Maison

From a pearl trader’s ledger to the Oscars red carpet

What began as a pearl trading house in 1952 grew into gold, then into fine diamond jewellery. Today Arzano is a supplier to royal households and private clients across the Middle East, Asia and the Americas. The craft has never changed: every stone is designed, cut, set and checked under one roof.

  1. 1952

    The Zaveri family establishes a pearl trading business.

  2. Gold → Diamonds

    The house moves into gold and then into high diamond jewellery.

  3. 2016

    Arzano clusters and cascades appear on the red carpet at the Emmy Awards.

  4. 2017

    Worn at the Academy Awards. Presented at Baselworld and JCK Luxury.

  5. 2026

    A new chapter: new collections, private viewings and a new digital home.
